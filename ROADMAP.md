# ROADMAP

## Major Structural Milestones
1. **Modernize Graphics Backend:** Decouple from legacy OpenGL to support Vulkan, Metal, and DirectX.
2. **Preset Parser Overhaul:** Develop a more robust parser for Milkdrop presets, supporting extended features and better error reporting.
3. **Cross-Platform Frontend Enhancements:** Ensure canonical frontends (SDL2, Rust) are feature-complete and robust.
4. **Performance Profiling:** Optimize FFT and beat detection algorithms for lower-end hardware and mobile devices.
